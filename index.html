VÆRNHEIM

Custom-fit car mats, moulded to each model

Vaernheim designs and sells made-to-measure floor mats and boot liners for passenger cars. The mats are moulded in TPE — a thermoplastic elastomer that stays flexible in Nordic winter temperatures, resists road salt, and is 100% recyclable. Each mat is shaped to the floor of a specific model, year and body variant, and locks into the car's original fixing points.

The brand is operated by Skogstjärnen AB, a Swedish company based in Karlskrona. Production is carried out by established moulding suppliers in the European Union. The online shop launches in autumn 2026.

Product range

  • Floor mats in TPE, front and rear, four mats per set
  • 3D liners with raised edges that hold melted snow and grit
  • Boot liners moulded per model

About this application

Vaernheim operates an internal reporting tool that connects to the Google Ads API. The tool reads performance data from the company's own Google Ads account in order to produce internal cost and performance reports. It does not access data belonging to any other party, and it publishes nothing to any third party.
