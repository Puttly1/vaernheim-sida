Custom-fit floor mats and boot liners in recyclable TPE, moulded to each car model.

Made in Europe 100% recyclable Launching autumn 2026

Moulded to one floor

Each mat is shaped to a scan of a specific model, year and body — following the tunnel, the pedal cut-out and the sill into the corners — and locks into the fixing points the car already has.

Made for the dark months

A raised edge holds meltwater, grit and road salt until you lift the mat out and hose it down. The material stays flexible far below freezing and does not crack when unfolded in frost.

Made in Europe

Moulded by established European suppliers under European environmental and labour rules, with short transport to the markets the mats are sold in.

Why TPE, and not rubber or textile

A textile mat soaks up the slush and returns it to the floor pan underneath. Rubber is heavy, stiffens in the cold and tends to smell in a warm cabin. TPE — a thermoplastic elastomer — does neither. It is lighter, stays supple in winter, has no scent of its own, and takes no colour from road salt.

It can also be melted down and moulded again without losing its properties. A worn set is raw material, not waste.
