Custom-fit floor mats and boot liners in recyclable TPE, moulded to each car model. Made in Europe.

Moulded to your car,
not to a template

Two cars sharing a model name can have different floors depending on year, body and drivetrain. Every Vaernheim mat is shaped to one specific floor — following the tunnel, the pedal cut-out and the sill all the way into the corners — and locks into the fixing points the car already has.

  • Exact fitShaped to a scan of the original floor, not to a generic outline.
  • Original fixingsNo loose clips or hook-and-loop strips that work themselves free.
  • Clear of the pedalsMaterial thickness matched to the pedal travel.

Made for
the dark months

A textile mat soaks up the slush and returns it to the floor pan underneath. TPE does the opposite: the raised edge holds the meltwater until you lift the mat out and hose it down.

The material stays flexible far below freezing, so it will not crack when unfolded in frost. It does not absorb road salt, leaves no white streaks, and has no scent of its own — so nothing needs perfuming to cover it.

Made in Europe,
and made to return

The mats are moulded by established European suppliers, under European environmental and labour rules, with short transport to the markets they are sold in.

TPE is a thermoplastic elastomer: it can be melted down and moulded again without losing its properties. A worn set is raw material, not waste.

About this application

Vaernheim operates an internal reporting tool that connects to the Google Ads API. The tool reads performance data from the company's own Google Ads account in order to produce internal cost and performance reports. It does not access data belonging to any other party, and it publishes nothing to any third party.
